  • 2026 - Present

    Independent Research (Sole Author)
    Pertura / PerturaBench
    An execution-grounded authority layer for LLM agents in Perturb-seq analysis.
    • Gated every scientific claim on verified execution, analysis scope, and upstream dependencies rather than on the agent’s own account of what it did.
    • Designed PerturaBench: 20 dependency-aware tasks over three Perturb-seq datasets and a donor-aware supplement, scoring 120 turns across three agent conditions and two prespecified repeats.
    • Prespecified the primary analysis and froze evaluators and thresholds before unblinding; reported clustered bootstrap confidence intervals and a sensitivity cohort, including the contrast that did not reproduce.
    • Reached 83.3% primary scientific fidelity vs. 44.4% and 38.9% for baselines, and 82.5% claim-authority success with no loss of informativeness.

    Cross-Tissue Single-Cell and Spatial Analysis of Sex and Hormone Effects in MASH.
